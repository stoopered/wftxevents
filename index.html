The Maze

What started as a routine harvest went wrong. Something got into the corn, and it's spreading. Walk the maze, keep your group together, and follow the flashlight beam — because whatever is out there in the stalks is faster than you think.

Live actors, fog, and a maze layout that changes every night. Not recommended for anyone who scares easily or cannot walk on uneven ground in the dark.
